# Hening — *The Solitude*

> **Olfactory Family:** Woody / French Lavender, Sandalwood & Cashmere Wood  
> **Concentration:** Eau de Parfum  
> **Character Archetype:** The Modern Contemplator

---

## 🎭 The Character

### **Persona**
Independent, internally grounded, and possessing a deep sense of inner peace. They do not require the noise of the outside world or constant validation to feel whole; silence is where they recharge and find their greatest strength. They love art, minimalist architecture, and contemplative nights.

### **Style & Presence**
*   **Clothing:** A minimalist high-quality wool coat in charcoal grey or architectural black, paired with a soft cashmere scarf.
*   **Gestures:** Graceful, unhurried movements, and a gaze that radiates deep clarity of mind.
*   **Aura:** Understated yet projecting a powerful quiet authority. Being near them immediately calms the chaos in others' minds.

---

## 📖 The Story

The city below has long been asleep, drowned in a thin blanket of mist during the night's final watch. In a minimalist studio with exposed concrete floors and a large window looking out onto silent pine trees, they remain awake. No music, no screens. Just pure silence filling the room.

They light a candle scented with wild **French Lavender** and **Sage** aromatic oil. The relaxing, fresh aromatic steam drifts slowly, clearing the mind from the remaining clutter of the day.

To them, silence is not an empty void or a scary loneliness. Silence is a priceless, luxurious canvas. In this moment, when the outside world turns off its volume, they can have an honest dialogue with their inner self.

They wrap a soft **Cashmere Wood** textured blanket around their shoulders, feeling its gentle warmth envelope their skin. As the night deepens toward dawn, the base notes of highly creamy, sweet, and sacred **Mysore Sandalwood** begin to waft from their pulse points. Rich sandalwood blends with pure **Amber**, creating an unshakeable sense of security.

Hening is their protector, an inner sanctuary they carry wherever they step in this noisy world.
