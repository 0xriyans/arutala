# Swarna — *The Romance*

> **Olfactory Family:** Woody / Mandarin, Benzoin & Warm Amber  
> **Concentration:** Eau de Parfum  
> **Character Archetype:** The Mature Romantic

---

## 🎭 The Character

### **Persona**
A warm, loving, sensual figure with a highly magnetic charisma. They are an incredible listener, valuing deep emotional connection over surface small talk. They believe in the beauty of mature love, loyalty, and protecting those they love.

### **Style & Presence**
*   **Clothing:** Soft knits or fine silk in warm terracotta, brick red, or copper brown that radiates a soft golden sheen under the sunlight.
*   **Gestures:** A warm and fully focused gaze when their partner speaks, and a reassuring touch on the shoulder or hand.
*   **Aura:** Intensely warm, safe, and alluring, like an embrace in front of a crackling log fire. They radiate emotional comfort that makes people feel accepted for who they are.

---

## 📖 The Story

Time seems to slow down and freeze. The afternoon sun fades into brushstrokes of orange, pink, and gold across the sky—a natural symphony known as the *Golden Hour*.

They sit side-by-side on the wooden porch sofa of an old pavilion, facing a rose garden in full bloom amidst a cool afternoon breeze. No heavy conversations, no devices interrupting their togetherness. Just an intimate, deep, and romantic silence.

They peel a sweet **Mandarin Orange**, letting the zesty essential oils evaporate into the air, blending with the vibrant warmth of **Pink Pepper**. The fresh citrus aroma brings a poetic brightness to the cooling afternoon air.

As they share the orange slice with their partner, their fingers touch firmly. An immense sense of security fills their chest—an emotional certainty that needs no grand promises or fleeting sweet words.

The surrounding air is slowly filled with the sweet honeyed aroma of golden **Benzoin** resin, **Cinnamon Bark**, and rich Madagascar vanilla. On their skin, **Warm Amber** radiates like the remaining warmth of the sunset embracing the earth. This is a scent of mature closeness, of gratitude for finding a place to rest amidst a tiring, noisy world. Swarna is the final harbor, where love is no longer a search, but a warm home.
