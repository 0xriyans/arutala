# Segara — *The Freedom*

> **Olfactory Family:** Woody / Sea Salt, Coastal Sage & Ambergris  
> **Concentration:** Eau de Parfum  
> **Character Archetype:** The Untethered Wanderer

---

## 🎭 The Character

### **Persona**
A free spirit, unbound by conventional societal limits, poetic, and loving wild adventures. They find inner satisfaction in getting lost in nature, listening to the roar of the ocean, and climbing isolated limestone cliffs. For them, material comfort is nothing compared to the luxury of pure freedom.

### **Style & Presence**
*   **Clothing:** A loose, rough-textured linen shirt with the top buttons undone, practical cargo trousers, and naturally wind-swept hair.
*   **Gestures:** A gaze always fixed on the distant horizon full of curiosity, a subtle mysterious yet friendly smile, and relaxed barefoot steps on the sand.
*   **Aura:** Free, slightly wild, salty-mineral, and deeply calming, like listening to waves crashing from afar that washes away all anxiety.

---

## 📖 The Story

They stand at the peak of a rugged limestone cliff at the southern tip of Bali, where the dry land ends and the deep blue Indian Ocean stretches infinitely toward Antarctica. Below, giant waves crash against ancient reef walls with a thunderous roar, sending sea spray tens of meters into the air.

The ocean breeze blows strongly, carrying cold **Sea Salt** particles and the fresh scent of the endless sky (**Ozone Accord**). They breathe in deeply, feeling the salty, crisp sensation burning their throat with pure relief.

Between the limestone crevices where they stand, wild coastal sage (**Coastal Sage**) and minty geranium grow thick, fighting the endless ocean wind. The sun-baked herbal aroma of sage blends with the salty sea spray, creating a beautiful contrast—a wild and honest botanical symphony.

Beneath their feet lies an old piece of **Driftwood** that has floated in the ocean for decades, bleached and hardened by salt and sun.

As the sun tilts west, the scent on their skin shifts to sweet, salty, warm, and deep **Ambergris**—a luxury only the vast ocean can create. Under the shifting orange-grey sky, they stretch out their hands. All mental fatigue, urban burnout, and future worries evaporate. They are completely free.
