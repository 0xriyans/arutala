# Senja — *The Dusk*

> **Olfactory Family:** Floral / Damascan Rose, Patchouli & Vanilla  
> **Concentration:** Eau de Parfum  
> **Character Archetype:** The Romantic Charmer

---

## 🎭 The Character

### **Persona**
Warm, artistic, and passionate. They appreciate beauty in everything—from classical poetry to abstract paintings. Their favorite moment is when day turns to night, when the boundary between reality and dream begins to fade. Senja is created for those who love with their entire soul.

### **Style & Presence**
*   **Clothing:** Clothes in warm colors like burgundy, wine red, plum, or tarnished gold, in soft silk or velvet fabrics.
*   **Gestures:** Full of gentleness, friendly, and highly expressive. Their smile invites instant comfort.
*   **Aura:** Romantic, sweet, alluring, and unforgettable.

---

## 📖 The Story

The sun begins to set slowly in the west, painting the sky with brushstrokes of orange, pink, and gold. The evening breeze brings a cooling air, marking the end of the day's hustle and the beginning of the night's warm embrace. **Senja** is a celebration of this golden hour (*golden hour*).

The sweet and fresh aroma of **Mandarin** and **Plum** welcomes you like soft afternoon sunlight. As the fragrance blends with the skin, **Damascan Rose**—the most romantic rose in the world—blooms with charm, merging with the sweetness of **Ylang-Ylang**.

When night fully falls, the remaining warmth is captured by a heady mix of sweet **Vanilla** and grounding **Patchouli**. This is the scent of a warm embrace from the one you love most.
