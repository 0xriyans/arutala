# 📖 Scent Stories

Every fragrance from **ARUTALA** is an emotional portrait. We craft scent chapters inspired by quiet architectures, fleeting natural light, and forgotten memories.

---

## 🪵 The Woody Collection
### Chapitre I: Les Bois Sacrés

> *"A low afternoon sun painting golden bands on library floors. The ancient silence of dry cedar and warm, bare skin."*

Our Woody collection is an architectural exploration of permanence. Composed of sustainable, cold-pressed Mysore Sandalwood, smoked Guaiacwood, and mystical Frankincense, these scents interact deeply with body chemistry to form an intensely intimate, warm aura that dries down to a velvety, quiet luxury.

* **Terroir:** Siena & Assam Highlands
* **Character:** Reverent, Earthy, Permanent
* **Sillages:** Mysore Sandalwood & Saffron

## 🌸 The Floral Collection
### Chapitre II: Les Fleurs Silencieuses

> *"Restraint in a loud world. A single, dewy bloom in a stone courtyard, wrapped in cashmere musks at the first light of dawn."*

Stripping away the heavy powder of classical bouquets, the Floral compositions celebrate single-note botanical integrity. By hand-harvesting Damascus Rose in Turkish valleys at daybreak and balancing it with wild Indian Tuberose, we construct airy, watercolors that smell of dew on grass, fresh green sap, and luxurious cashmere linens.

* **Terroir:** Java Garden & Nusantara
* **Character:** Candid, Dewy, Whispering
* **Sillages:** Damascus Rose & White Musk

### 📸 Image Generation Prompt - Floral Collection Mood
**Visual Concept:** Foto produk minimalis editorial bernuansa bunga. Pencahayaan matahari pagi yang lembut dari samping menghasilkan bayangan bunga mawar yang elegan.

## 🍋 The Citrus Collection
### Chapitre III: Les Hespéridées

> *"Sunshine distilled. Crisp, green petitgrain leaves crushed between fingers, with a salty Mediterranean breeze lifting the spirit."*

Our Citrus collection is a celebration of luminous, fleeting joy. Formulated as rich Eaux de Cologne Intenses, we pair cold-pressed Sicilian lemons and bitter Calabrian bergamot with creamy orange blossoms and mineral sea salt, creating a sparkling, clean, and radiant signature that floats effortlessly.

* **Terroir:** Bali Coast & Nusantara
* **Character:** Luminous, Salty, Joyous
* **Sillages:** Amalfi Lemon & Orange Blossom

### 📸 Image Generation Prompt - Citrus Collection Mood
