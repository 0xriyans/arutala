# ⏳ Chronology of Scent: The Scent Journey

*Witness the evolution of our olfactory architecture. From a single copper still in our Jakarta laboratory to Indonesia's premier botanical house, explore our signature epochs.*

## 2018: Genesis
### Rintik (The Genesis in Jakarta)

A liquid memory of dry, parched earth instantly refreshed by the very first droplets of rain after a long monsoon dry season. It unfolds with clean, ozonic mineral notes, settling into a comforting heart of warm jasmine and calming green tea.

* **Concentration:** Trial Run (Eau de Parfum - 18% pure oils)
* **Olfactory Profile:** Petrichor, Green Tea, Jasmine

### 📸 Image Generation Prompt - Rintik
**Visual Concept:** Foto produk studio minimalis elegan. Botol parfum kaca silinder transparan 100ml berdesain bersih dengan bagian bawah tebal (*weighted bottom*) dan tutup hitam matte minimalis, berlabel merk **"Arutala"** dengan nama varian **"Rintik"** di bawahnya menggunakan jenis font Inter sans-serif geometris yang bersih dan modern. Botol diletakkan di atas balok batu travertine putih bersih bertekstur halus dengan latar belakang polos berwarna gading hangat (*warm ivory*). Pencahayaan lembut satu arah menghasilkan bayangan bersih yang anggun.
**Prompt:**
> *High-end minimalist product photography of a sleek 100ml cylindrical clear glass perfume bottle with a heavy weighted bottom and a clean matte black cap, printed with the brand name "ARUTALA" and the product name "Rintik" in elegant clean Inter sans-serif geometric typography. The bottle sits centered on a solid, clean off-white travertine stone block. Pure, solid warm-ivory studio background. Soft diffused side lighting, crisp clean shadows, ultra-minimalist, 8k.*

## 2020: The First Opus
### Segara (The First Signature Opus)

The debut of our first public offering. Segara captured the hearts of perfume connoisseurs globally by redefining marine ambergris. We removed the heavy, sweet notes, and instead paired skin-warmed sea salt with raw grey ambergris and coastal sage.

* **Concentration:** Eau de Parfum (18% pure oils)
* **Olfactory Profile:** Sea Salt, Coastal Sage, Ambergris

### 📸 Image Generation Prompt - Segara
**Visual Concept:** Foto produk mewah minimalis bernuansa laut. Botol silinder kaca tebal dengan dasar riak air yang memantulkan cahaya, dan tutup kayu driftwood abu-abu alami dengan serat kayu yang detail. Botol diletakkan secara terpusat di atas bongkahan batu karang putih kasar (*sea-weathered white coral rock*) dengan latar belakang gradasi biru laut dalam ke abu-abu berkabut yang tenang. Efek pencahayaan sinematik dengan bayangan dramatis layaknya sinar matahari sore menerobos air laut.
**Prompt:**
> *High-end commercial product shot of a cylindrical clear glass perfume bottle with a water-ripple textured bottom and a raw grey driftwood cap, printed with the brand "ARUTALA" and "Segara" in sleek organic Inter sans-serif typography. The bottle is resting on a coarse, white sea-weathered coral rock against a soft, gradient deep coastal blue to misty grey studio background. Golden hour sunbeams filtering through water, casting caustic light reflections and long, dramatic shadows. Minimalist, ultra-luxury aesthetic, photorealistic, 8k.*

## 2024: Modern Craft
### Puspa (Captured at 5:00 AM)

An opulent, mysterious, and carnal white floral scent celebrating the magical night-blooming flowers of Indonesia. Heavy, hand-harvested tuberose and rich, dark roses are grounded by a deep patchouli and black musk foundation.

* **Concentration:** Extrait de Parfum (22% pure oils)
* **Olfactory Profile:** Jasmin Sambac, Tuberose, Patchouli

### 📸 Image Generation Prompt - Puspa
**Visual Concept:** Foto produk studio minimalis misterius. Botol parfum silinder ramping 100ml dari kaca hitam asap semi-transparan dengan tutup kubus obsidian hitam mengilap, berlabel merk **"Arutala"** dengan nama varian **"Puspa"** di bawahnya menggunakan jenis font Cormorant Garamond serif bernuansa Javanese gothic yang eksotis, misterius, indah, dan bersudut elegan dengan tinta perak tipis. Botol diletakkan di atas balok batu granit hitam yang dipoles halus dengan latar belakang polos hitam pekat (*solid matte black*). Pencahayaan tunggal berdensitas tinggi meniru cahaya bulan menghasilkan bayangan kontras tinggi yang dramatis dan bersih.
**Prompt:**
> *High-end minimalist product shot of a slender 100ml semi-transparent smoked black glass cylindrical perfume bottle with a polished black obsidian square cap, printed with the brand name "ARUTALA" and the product name "Puspa" in elegant, Javanese gothic-inspired silver Cormorant Garamond serif typography underneath. The bottle sits centered on a clean, polished black granite block against a solid matte black studio background. Dramatic single spotlight mimicking silver moonlight, sharp high-contrast clean shadows, mystical minimalist, 8k.*
