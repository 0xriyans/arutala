# Kala — *The Time*

> **Olfactory Family:** Woody / Agarwood, Incense & Black Pepper  
> **Concentration:** Extrait de Parfum  
> **Character Archetype:** The Timeless Master

---

## 🎭 The Character

### **Persona**
A mysterious, calm, yet undeniably authoritative figure. They respect tradition, silence, and have a deep understanding of the value of time. Kala is crafted for those who leave an eternal impression wherever they go.

### **Style & Presence**
*   **Clothing:** Classic tailored premium dark clothing—such as black, charcoal, or deep maroon.
*   **Gestures:** Calm, slow, and deliberate. A sharp and deep gaze reflecting wisdom.
*   **Aura:** Masculine, deep, mysterious, and full of authority.

---

## 📖 The Story

In the middle of the constantly moving flow of time, there is a point where everything seems to stand still. Incense smoke slowly rises, carrying spiritual peace from the past. **Kala** is a tribute to these eternal moments.

A zesty burst of **Black Pepper** at the opening symbolizes the spark of life, immediately followed by the magical embrace of slow-burning **Incense**. The main foundation of this perfume is rare and precious **Agarwood (Oud)**, providing an unfathomable depth.

Kala is not just a perfume; it is a time capsule. When you wear it, you do not just wear a fragrance, but history and mystery that has lived for thousands of years.
